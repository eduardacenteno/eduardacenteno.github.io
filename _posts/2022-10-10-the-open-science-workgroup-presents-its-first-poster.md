Our [Open Science Workgroup](https://anatomy-neurosciences.com/initiatives/openscience/) showcased its first poster at the National Open Science Festival, hosted at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam in the Netherlands. This great event allowed us to connect with like-minded individuals and expand our network. Our poster even caught the attention of the VU Rector Magnificus!! Explore the festival highlights [here](https://twitter.com/eduardagzc/status/1566695461804802049/photo/1) & stay tuned for more updates as we continue promoting Open Science practices.
