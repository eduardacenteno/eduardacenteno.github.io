Last week, I had the great opportunity to give a workshop on how to write clean and readable code at the [ORPER summer school](https://orperschool.github.io/) at the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology in Lausanne (EPFL). Furthermore, I participated in a panel discussion with [Russ Poldrack](https://profiles.stanford.edu/russell-poldrack), [Zoltan Dienes](https://profiles.sussex.ac.uk/p718-zoltan-dienes), and [Juliette Boscheron](https://people.epfl.ch/juliette.boscheron) (event organizer & a good friend) about changing the research incentives toward Open Science values.
