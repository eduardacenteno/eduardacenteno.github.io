The [Open Science Working Group](https://anatomy-neurosciences.com/initiatives/openscience/) from the Anatomy and Neurosciences department hit the jackpot! We applied and were selected for the 8th cohort of the [OLS training program](https://openlifesci.org/openseeds/ols-8/projects-participants.html). *Drum roll, please!* And what's more exciting? We won a whopping 3K from the [VU Challenge Award](https://vu.nl/en/news/2023/vu-open-science-challenge-award-2023-eight-projects-funded) to work on our super-duper project: **Crafting a Handbook for Open Science in Neuroscience** 🎉🎉🎉
