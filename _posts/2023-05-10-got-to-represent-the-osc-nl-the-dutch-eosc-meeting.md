I was invited to represent the Open Science Communities in the Netherlands at the Dutch EOSC Tripartite meeting at SURF (Utrecht) earlier this year. Alongside other panelists, I advocated for the sustained funding of these communities to facilitate the implementation of Open Science practices in research. You can find more information about the event [here](https://osc-international.com/inosc/osc-nl-at-the-dutch-eosc-tripartite-event/).
